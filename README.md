# clubManager
Unified command centre for high-performing co-curricular organisations.

## Strategic Focus
clubManager is purpose-built for executive committees that scale fast, operate across multiple workstreams, and demand measurable impact from every initiative. By replacing fragmented spreadsheets and chat threads with a single source of truth, the platform keeps governance tight, communications precise, and decision cycles short.

> Crafted by a two-person product squad within a 12-week build window, the platform demonstrates how lean teams can ship enterprise-grade capabilities with disciplined execution.

## Platform Capabilities
- **Multi-channel Communications**: Orchestrate announcement lifecycles with segmentation, automated reminders, and read-receipt telemetry.
- **Insight-driven Feedback**: Deploy polls, pulse checks, and sentiment analysis to capture member voice and inform rapid pivots.
- **Secure Knowledge Vault**: Curate constitutions, SOPs, and creative assets in permissioned workspaces backed by audit trails and version history.
- **People Intelligence Graph**: Map member profiles, leadership pipelines, competencies, and tenure to strengthen succession planning.
- **Operational Playbooks**: Coordinate event plans, task assignments, and budget approvals with dependency tracking and SLA monitoring.
- **Executive Analytics**: Visualise engagement KPIs, resource utilisation, and forecasted workloads through interactive dashboards and exports.
